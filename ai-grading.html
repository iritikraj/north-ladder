How it works

The 20-second AR
grading process

1.Device scanned by AR camera

Partner uses the NorthLadder grading app. Camera scans device in a continuous 20-second motion, capturing front, back, edges, ports, screen, and physical condition from all angles.

2.AI assigns grade automatically

NorthLadder's computer vision model analyses the scan against the standardised rubric. Grade A–D assigned in real time. IMEI confirmed via hardware read at point of scan.

3.Certificate + video stored per IMEI

The grading video and certificate are stored against the device IMEI. Buyers see the link in the auction lot detail. If a dispute arises, the video is the objective record — no buyer-vs-seller argument.

4.Lot enters auction with grade-backed SLA

Every lot that goes to auction carries the AR grade and a grade-backed SLA commitment. If device arrives at a lower grade than auctioned, NorthLadder's dispute process applies the video evidence.
