The Auction Process

From submission
to settlement.

1 Partner submits lot

Partner uploads device inventory — IMEI, model, volume — via API or portal. Lot scheduled for grading and auction. Details visible to buyers immediately.

2 AR video grading

Each device receives a 20-second AR video grade at point of collection. Grade A–D assigned. Video stored on record per IMEI.

3 Live open auction

All verified buyers bid simultaneously. No reserve prices unless specified. Countdown timer. Auto-bid available. Bid history visible.

4 Settlement & logistics

Winning buyer receives invoice immediately. Payment due in 48h. Logistics arranged directly or via NL-coordinated collection.

Why we publish every fee

NorthLadder charges the same rate to every buyer and every seller — and publishes it. This is what "neutral marketplace" means in practice. You never pay a hidden margin because NL never holds inventory or acts as a buyer itself.
