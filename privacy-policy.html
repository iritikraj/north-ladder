Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Contents

1. Introduction 2. Information We Collect 3. How We Use Your Information 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure 5. Data Security & Retention 6. Your Privacy Rights 7. Contact Us

1. Introduction

NorthLadder ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our auction platform, AR grading application, or engage with our services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, references, is capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular consumer or device ("personal information").

  • Business Account Data: Name, business email address, corporate entity details, phone number, and billing information when you register as a buyer or partner.
  • Device & Grading Data: When utilizing our AR application, we collect device IMEI numbers, hardware diagnostics, and 20-second AR video captures of physical devices. Note: This data pertains to the hardware being auctioned and does not contain personal user data from the device's previous owner, as all devices must be factory wiped prior to grading.
  • Usage & Analytics: IP addresses, browser types, interaction with auction lots, bidding history, and platform navigation metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected data to operate a neutral, transparent marketplace and provide our SLA-backed services:

  • To facilitate the bidding, grading, and settlement processes within the NorthLadder ecosystem.
  • To maintain the integrity of our AR grading standard and resolve disputes using objective video records.
  • To aggregate anonymized market data for the Residual Value (RV) Index.
  • To comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

  • With Platform Participants: Limited corporate information and device grading certificates are shared between relevant buyers and sellers to facilitate transactions.
  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party vendors (e.g., payment processors, cloud hosting providers like AWS) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. All AR grading videos and certificates are encrypted at rest and in transit.

We retain personal information and transaction data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, resolve disputes, and comply with our legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including the EU/EEA under GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer using the details below.
