Why "neutral" matters in the architecture

NorthLadder's platform is structurally incapable of buying inventory. There is no internal buyer account. No allocation layer that bypasses auction.

The core platform runs open bidding — the same interface for every verified buyer — because the architecture enforces neutrality, not just policy.
