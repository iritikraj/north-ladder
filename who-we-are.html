Inside NorthLadder

Our story and
what drives us

NorthLadder was founded in Dubai with a simple observation: the secondary device market was full of single-buyer pricing spreads, photo-based grading disputes, and intermediaries that profited by holding information asymmetry between partners and buyers. The market was large. The infrastructure was broken.

We built the platform to fix it. An AI grading standard that replaced 7 subjective photos with a 20-second objective AR video. An open auction where every buyer sees every bid. A neutral infrastructure that cannot buy inventory — and therefore cannot manipulate prices. Published fees. Grade-backed SLAs.

Today, 75+ partners across EU, GCC, and Asia connect their trade-in inventory to NorthLadder. €Xm in devices traded. 94% lot sell-through. 40% fewer disputes. We're based in Dubai with operations across Europe and the Gulf — and we're building the data layer that the circular device economy needs.
